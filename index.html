A Simulation - Based Pedagogical Framework for Teaching Linear and Quadratic Discriminant Analysis
Developed an interactive, simulation-driven teaching framework to explain Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) and Quadratic Discriminant Analysis (QDA) — two foundational classification techniques in statistical machine learning. The material uses simulated datasets to visually and intuitively demonstrate how decision boundaries are formed, how class distributions influence predictions, and where each method performs best. Designed to bridge the gap between mathematical theory and practical understanding, making complex statistical concepts accessible to learners at an introductory to intermediate level.

02
Irreducible Elements in a Ring
Developed teaching material exploring the concept of irreducible elements within abstract algebra, specifically in the context of ring theory. The material covers the formal definition of irreducibility, its distinction from prime elements, and how these concepts generalize familiar notions of prime numbers to broader algebraic structures. Designed to build rigorous mathematical intuition, the content guides learners through key theorems, illustrative examples across various ring types — such as integers, polynomial rings, and Gaussian integers — and the role irreducibility plays in understanding unique factorization domains (UFDs).
